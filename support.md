# Direct Play Support

Direct Play is an Apple TV app for browsing and playing media from a Plex server you control.

## Contact

Support contact: [open an issue on the Direct Play support repo](https://github.com/briankennedy1/direct-play-support/issues).

## Before Contacting Support

- Confirm your Apple TV and Plex server are on the same network.
- Confirm the Plex server URL in Settings includes the scheme and port, for example `http://192.168.0.235:32400`.
- Re-link your Plex account in Settings if the app says Plex rejected the token.
- Refresh the Plex library if the app says a media part is unavailable.
- Confirm the file still exists on the NAS or storage path used by Plex.

## What Direct Play Does Not Provide

Direct Play does not provide movies, TV shows, live channels, or hosted media. It only connects to the Plex server you configure and plays media you already own or have permission to access.

Direct Play is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Plex.
