Direct Play is an Apple TV app for browsing and playing media from a Plex server you configure.

Data Stored On Your Device

Direct Play stores your Plex access token in Keychain, a stable Direct Play client identifier in UserDefaults, and your configured Plex server URL in UserDefaults.

Network Connections

Direct Play connects to the Plex server URL you configure to browse and play your media library. It also contacts plex.tv during Plex link-code sign-in. During playback, Direct Play reports playback timeline state to your configured Plex server so resume and watched status can update.

Media Library Data

Direct Play reads metadata from your configured Plex server, including show, season, episode, artwork, file, codec, duration, resume, and playback metadata. Direct Play does not upload your media library metadata to any service other than Plex endpoints chosen by you.

Tracking And Advertising

Direct Play does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Direct Play does not sell personal information.

Media Content

Direct Play does not provide, host, sell, or index movies, TV shows, live channels, or other media content. You are responsible for using Direct Play only with media you own or have permission to access.
